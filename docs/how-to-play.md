# How to play Chase the Cat

Pickles the cat swiped the Golden Squeaky. Four dogs race to tree her. Be the first one there.

## Steering

| You do this | Your dog does this |
| --- | --- |
| Tip the phone forward | Runs |
| Lean left or right | Steers |
| Tip forward and left | Runs forward and left |
| Hold the phone in your normal spot | Stays |
| Tip the phone back | Backs up |
| Tap the screen (tilt) or hit ZOOM (thumb) | Zoomies |

The way you are holding the phone when the countdown starts is your stay spot. If it drifts, pause and tap **Reset my stay position**.

Rather not tilt? Choose **Thumb** before the race and drag anywhere on the screen. On a computer, use the arrow keys and the space bar.

## On the track

- **Bones** are worth points and a little energy.
- **Water** slows you down. Bridges cross the streams.
- **Hedges, walls and crates** stop you. Find the gap.
- **Kids** hold their arms out for a hug. Get close and your dog goes over on his own. A hug takes about a second, pays points and Love, and gives you a Happy Tail speed boost. Each kid hugs each dog once.
- **The cat** runs ahead. Now and then she stops and taunts. Touch her before she bolts for a bonus. You will usually need Zoomies to reach her in time.

## Bad Owners

Each one is mean to dogs in his own way. Run into one to eat him.

| Bad Owner | What he does | How to get him |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Hoser | Sweeps a hose jet across the road | Wait for the jet to swing away, then run up the edge |
| The Snatcher | Swings a net at any dog in front of him | Make him miss, then go in while he untangles |
| The Chainer | Stretches a chain across the road | Go around, or eat him and the chain drops for everyone |
| The Yeller | Blasts a ring of noise | Rush in right after a ring passes |
| The Boomer | Throws firecrackers | Leave the red circle before it pops |

Eating a Bad Owner gives big points, a full pip of energy, and frees a puppy that follows you. Eat several in a row without getting hit and the points grow.

Every fifth level ends with a boss behind a locked gate. Bite him three times (five for the last one) and the gate opens.

## Zoomies

The three circles at the bottom are your energy. One full circle buys one burst of Zoomies: much faster for a couple of seconds, not slowed by water, and nothing a Bad Owner does can touch you. Your bark at the start of the burst also stuns Bad Owners nearby.

## Scoring

| For | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Bone | 50 |
| Hug | 300 |
| Bad Owner eaten | 2,500 (1.5 times for the second in a row, double from the third) |
| Boss bite | 5,000, plus 5,000 for the last bite |
| Cat tag | 750 |
| Each pup rescued | 200 |
| Finishing 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th | 5,000, 3,000, 2,000, 1,000 |
| Each second under par time | 100 |
| Clean run (never hit by a Bad Owner) | 1,500 |

Later levels pay more: every level after the first adds 5 percent, so level 20 pays almost double. A higher-level dog also earns 2 percent more per level.

## Stars and levels

- One star: finish in the top three. That opens the next level.
- Two stars: win.
- Three stars: win and beat the par time.

## Your dog's level

Love comes mostly from hugs. Enough Love levels your dog up, to a top level of 20. Each level makes that dog a touch faster and makes his scores count for more. Every dog levels up on its own.

## Friend races

Tap **Race Friends**, then **Start a pack**. Read the four-letter code to your friends, or tap **Share invite**. They open the game, tap **Race Friends**, type the code and tap **Join pack**. Up to four phones can join. Computer dogs fill any empty spots. The player who started the pack picks the level and starts the race.
